01 — The Problem

Spreadsheets, Paper, and the Awkward Money Chat

The "Chase" Fatigue: Freelancers and small business owners often feel like "nags" when following up on unpaid invoices. This creates emotional stress and delays essential cash flow, as they lack a professional, automated way to remind clients of upcoming or overdue payments without damaging the client relationship.

The Admin "Gap": Many business owners wait until the end of the month to handle billing because current tools are too complex or desktop-oriented. This "delayed invoicing" leads to forgotten billable hours and lost revenue, as the friction of requesting payment in the moment—especially for those with busy schedules or accessibility needs—is too high.

02 — The Solution

Simple, Frictionless Tracking and Automated Reminders

To provide users with real-time visibility into the status of their payments (e.g., "Sent," "Viewed," "Paid") and automate the follow-up process. The goal is to eliminate the manual "checking and chasing" work, giving the business owner back at least 2–3 hours of administrative time per week.

The idea was simple. Enable the user to send the customer a payment request when the job has been completed. The app automatically logs when that request was sent and sets up automatic reminders if the payment request remains unpaid.

03 — How I Got There

Testing with the Trio and Visual Reality Checks

I tested with three people - a freelance photographer, a self employed electrician and a designer. All 3 liked the initial designs but were adamant that the interface needed to be clear and agreed that the payment requests needed to be shown clearly in the app in two simple states - Paid (in green) and Unpaid (in red).

One of the more elderly testers also made the point that when something happened it needed to be really clear - hence the big ticks after a confirmation screen.
